How it works

A simple path into the Alliance.

Partnership is organization-based, currently free, and designed to be collaborative rather than burdensome. We are looking for organizations that share the Alliance's mission and are actively contributing to springs conservation or helping enable conservation action.

01 · Apply

Tell us about your organization, where you work, and how your work connects to springs.

02 · Review

The Alliance reviews applications for alignment with its mission, principles, and global community.

03 · Connect

Approved organizations join the partner network and can begin connecting with Alliance opportunities and initiatives.
