Our Initiatives

The Global Springs Alliance is a permanent platform for collaboration and action. Through focused initiatives, the Alliance brings partners together to address major needs and opportunities for springs conservation around the world.

IUCN Global Springs Task Force

The first major initiative of the Global Springs Alliance brings together global expertise to build the scientific and conservation foundation needed to advance springs conservation worldwide.

What's next

More initiatives are coming.

The IUCN Global Springs Task Force is the first of many initiatives envisioned for the Global Springs Alliance. As the Alliance grows, new initiatives will respond to priorities identified by the global springs community and create opportunities for collaboration, conservation action, capacity building, restoration, investment, and stewardship.
