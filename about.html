About the Alliance

Stronger together for springs.

The Global Springs Alliance is a global network bringing together organizations and partners to protect, restore, and sustainably steward springs and spring-dependent species.

Why an Alliance

Great work is happening around the world. Too often, it happens in isolation.

The challenge is not simply to create more projects. It is to connect the people and institutions already carrying knowledge, experience, influence, resources, and stewardship capacity.

Scientists are advancing our understanding of springs. Communities are protecting places that sustain livelihoods, traditions, and cultures. Governments and conservation organizations are restoring watersheds and managing groundwater. Practitioners are developing new ways to identify, monitor, restore, and steward springs.

Yet much of this work remains fragmented across disciplines, organizations, countries, and regions. Knowledge does not always reach the people who can use it. Successful approaches can remain local. Organizations may lack access to technical expertise, partners, or funding.

The Global Springs Alliance exists to make those connections — strengthening and amplifying existing organizations and initiatives rather than replacing them.

A global community

Different places. Different expertise. One shared future for springs.

How we work

Connect. Enable. Transform.

01

Connect

Bring organizations, practitioners, researchers, governments, Indigenous Peoples and local communities, funders, and partners together around shared challenges and opportunities.

02

Enable

Strengthen access to knowledge, skills, tools, technical support, partnerships, and resources so people can conserve springs more effectively.

03

Transform

Turn collaboration and capacity into conservation action that can be strengthened, replicated, scaled, and reflected in policy, practice, investment, and public awareness.

Built to collaborate

A platform, not a replacement.

The Alliance is designed to build on the organizations, networks, initiatives, and expertise that already exist. Its role is to make the whole greater than the sum of its parts.

That means helping people find one another, share knowledge, develop partnerships, strengthen capacity, mobilize resources, and work together on challenges that no single organization can effectively tackle alone.

Our vision
A world where springs are recognized, valued, protected, and restored as vital ecosystems — and where a connected global community provides a strong collective voice for their conservation.
Who belongs

Springs conservation needs a bigger table.

The Alliance is being built for conservation organizations, Indigenous Peoples and community-based organizations, researchers and universities, governments, protected-area managers, zoos and conservation institutions, foundations, private-sector partners, professional associations, and others contributing directly to springs conservation.
