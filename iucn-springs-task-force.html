Initiative 01 · IUCN Global Springs Task Force

Building the global foundation for springs conservation.

An inter-Commission effort bringing together expertise across disciplines and regions to advance springs conservation worldwide.

The work

Define & map. Assess. Prioritize. Guide.

Define & Map

Build common definitions and improve the global picture of where springs occur.

Assess

Advance consistent approaches to biodiversity, ecological condition, and protection.

Prioritize & Guide

Identify conservation priorities and translate knowledge into practical guidance.

Why now

Small ecosystems. Outsized importance.

Springs support distinctive biodiversity and connect groundwater, surface water, and terrestrial systems, yet many remain poorly documented and increasingly pressured.

Four-year program

From foundations to capacity.

Year 1: Foundations & Mapping
Year 2: Data & Assessment
Year 3: Prioritization & Strategy
Year 4: Guidance & Capacity
