How it works

A simple path into the coalition.

The Expert Coalition brings together people with relevant scientific, technical, practical, cultural, policy, communications, and conservation expertise. Applications help us understand your background, connection to springs or freshwater conservation, and how you would like to contribute to the Global Springs Alliance.

01 · Apply

Tell us about your expertise, experience, geographic focus, and connection to springs or freshwater conservation.

02 · Review

The Alliance reviews applications for relevant expertise, alignment with the coalition’s purpose, and opportunities for meaningful participation.

03 · Connect

Accepted members can be featured on the Expert Coalition page and connected with Alliance initiatives, collaborations, and opportunities.
